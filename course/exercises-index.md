# Course item and exercise index

The table maps detailed topics from pages 4 and 5 to their own directories. A reference such as `14.2` means item two in week fourteen. General weekly headings are not additional exercises, and reading topics are not automatically practical labs.

There are 13 weekly practical assessments in weeks 4 to 16; that is not the count of detailed exercises. Embedded systems has four separate integration exercises: Arduino, STM32, Raspberry Pi and Odroid across weeks 14 to 16. Item 16.3 is review and week 17 is the final assessment. Item 6.2 groups RViz and package building, and item 7.2 groups Gazebo setup and URDF/SDF conversion; implementation can split their steps without changing syllabus references. Extensions have a separate catalog. Planned entries do not imply completed code.
